## What the documents are

- **FBI-UAP-D026** (`4ea340f612c3f8c2`): a three-page FD-302 interview report, titled "Dark Translucent Triangle," 2023, from an interview in January 2026. A digital rendering, D027, is a separate card.
- **FBI-UAP-D030** (`96f4d5e8c8f90ee5`): a three-page FD-302, titled "Large Triangle with Red Lights," 2023, from an interview in October 2025.
- **LLE-UAP-D002** (`247e6e1bd6da7377`): a transcript of a 15-minute video, LLE-UAP-PR002, taken by a law enforcement officer in Colorado in October 2023.
- **LLE-UAP-D004** (`b1d24cb7f46cfa20`): a transcript of a 1-minute, 42-second video, LLE-UAP-PR004, taken in Colorado in January 2024.
- **LLE-UAP-D001** (`88443d8d0c5b0c44`) and D003 (`31656bd3e7de783d`) are the transcripts of two shorter clips. D001 has three lines of speech, none of them describing anything, and D003 has no discernible dialogue. We list D001 because the release does, and quote nothing from either.

The FBI reports are heavily redacted. Names, addresses and the interviewers are blacked out, and we do not try to identify the witnesses or their locations. The video transcripts were edited by AARO, which removed the audio and redacted the reporter's identity. Each FBI page ends with the line:

> "This document contains neither recommendations nor conclusions of the FBI." <Cite card="4ea340f612c3f8c2" page={1} q="This document contains neither recommendations nor conclusions of the FBI." />

## The first FBI account: four seconds over a porch

In D026 a man and his wife were on a back porch in Colorado Springs at about 10:50 PM in October 2023, and he noticed something had flown over the house. He put it low, "possibly as low as 300 feet", and roughly the size of a large SUV <Cite card="4ea340f612c3f8c2" page={2} q="The UAP appeared low in elevation, possibly as low as 300 feet." />. The report says that when he first spotted it

> "first spotted the UAP he thought it appeared almost see-through, and it had" what appeared to be a haze or distortion around it. <Cite card="4ea340f612c3f8c2" page={2} q="first spotted the UAP he thought it appeared almost see-through, and it had" />

He compared the view to looking through mist, with "ripples like you were looking at an object that is underwater" <Cite card="4ea340f612c3f8c2" page={2} q="ripples like you were looking at an object that is underwater." />. It looked like a perfect triangle, with a band of silverish-blue light along the trailing edge. As it left, the left side "banked up quickly to an almost vertical orientation", though it did not change path or elevation. The report says the object "was completely silent" <Cite card="4ea340f612c3f8c2" page={2} q="The UAP was completely silent" /> and that he saw it for a total of four seconds.

The witness describes himself as very familiar with Air Force aircraft, and says the object did not match anything he had encountered <Cite card="4ea340f612c3f8c2" page={3} q="The UAP he and his wife observed did not match anything he" />. That is his claim, as the FBI wrote it down. The report does not test it. He also notes that the distortion around the object may have made it look translucent, and may have softened its edges.

## The second FBI account: a cloud that wasn't a cloud

D030 is a different man, at a different place in the same city, on an October 2023 evening between 8 and 9:30 PM, interviewed in October 2025. He saw what looked like a dark-gray cloud "phasing into his sight" <Cite card="96f4d5e8c8f90ee5" page={2} q="colored cloud phasing into his sight." />, stationary, as if it was "pixelated or distorted" <Cite card="96f4d5e8c8f90ee5" page={2} q="as if it was pixelated or distorted" />. A solid black triangle with a red light in each corner came out of it. He estimated it could have been as large as the parking lot he was standing in. It first moved at helicopter speed, then

> "It then almost instantaneously accelerated to possibly Mach 1 or 2 and" turned slightly. <Cite card="96f4d5e8c8f90ee5" page={2} q="It then almost instantaneously accelerated to possibly Mach 1 or 2 and" />

He saw waves around it like a mirage over a hot road, heard no noise, and saw it bank 90 degrees to one side and back. The report adds that the banking was in the opposite sense from a conventional aircraft's turn: "was opposite of a conventional aircraft would bank in the direction it was" turning <Cite card="96f4d5e8c8f90ee5" page={2} q="was opposite of a conventional aircraft would bank in the direction it was" />. One more detail is in the report. He called his wife about 60 seconds later, and she told him that his voice "sounded robotic, as if there was" electronic interference <Cite card="96f4d5e8c8f90ee5" page={2} q="she said that his voice sounded robotic, as if there was" />. That is his wife's remark as he relayed it, in one telephone call, and nothing else in the report tests it.

## The law-enforcement videos

The two video transcripts are first-person narration by an officer. Each carries the releaser's note that "All descriptive and estimative language in this transcript reflects the reporter's subjective" interpretation at the time <Cite card="b1d24cb7f46cfa20" page={1} q="All descriptive and estimative language in this transcript reflects the reporter's subjective" />.

In D002 (October 2023, 15 minutes), the speaker describes watching a light for a second night and says "It's the second night in a row" <Cite card="247e6e1bd6da7377" page={1} q="It's the second night in a row." />. He says that they stay for hours and "will fly in triangular motions" <Cite card="247e6e1bd6da7377" page={2} q="Weird thing about these things is they stay there for hours, and they will fly in triangular motions." />, and that, at one point, it looks like "two distinct lights in that orb" <Cite card="247e6e1bd6da7377" page={2} q="It's like two distinct lights in that orb." />. He also says one of them had shined a bright red and blue beam into his patrol vehicle the day before <Cite card="247e6e1bd6da7377" page={2} q="bright red and blue beam into my truck, a patrol vehicle." />.

In D004 (January 2024, a different night and a different roadside), the speaker says he is watching "another orb" about 300 feet up <Cite card="b1d24cb7f46cfa20" page={1} q="300 feet up off the ground." />, that he checked radar and "There is no aircraft flying in the area" <Cite card="b1d24cb7f46cfa20" page={1} q="There is no aircraft flying in the area" />, and that "This has been flying stationary for about 15 minutes." <Cite card="b1d24cb7f46cfa20" page={1} q="This has been flying stationary for about 15 minutes." />

The radar check is the speaker's statement. The transcript does not say what radar, or what it could and could not see at 300 feet. The videos themselves (PR001 to PR004) are separate video cards with no text, and this entry quotes only the transcripts.

## How these relate

They share a state and a season. We link them for that reason, and the reason is thin:

- The two FBI interviews describe two different triangles, from two different people, who were interviewed in 2025 and 2026, two to three years after the events. Neither mentions the law-enforcement videos.
- The video transcripts describe orbs and lights, not triangles. D004 is from January 2024, not October 2023.
- The earlier entry on [the Western U.S. event of October 2023](/finds/western-united-states-event-october-2023) covers six federal agents and orbs in a western state. None of the documents in this entry says it concerns that event, and none rules it out. It is a hypothesis worth checking against case files, not a finding.

## What we're not claiming

We are not claiming these accounts describe one event, a single object, or a pattern. We are also not claiming any of them was anything unusual. The FBI reports record what witnesses said, years afterward, with the identities and places redacted. A triangle seen for four seconds at night against a clear sky, or an unfamiliar light near an airport, can be misjudged for size, distance and speed, and the reports do not give a way to check the estimates. The remark about the phone call is one person's account of another person's remark. Every quotation above is verbatim from the released OCR, pinned to its card and page. The selection and framing are ours.
