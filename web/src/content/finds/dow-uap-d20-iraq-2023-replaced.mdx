- **Landing:** `010148ZAPR23`. Mission duration approximately 6 hours 47 minutes.
- **Routing:** transited Iraqi airspace, then Syrian airspace; on station in killbox "ESSA" from 2058Z to 0000Z.
- **UAP event DTG:** `312302:05ZMAR23` — 31 March 2023, 23:02 Zulu.
- **Observation:** approximately 10–20 bright objects moving west-to-east at flight level above 600 (the F-16s were at FL265). Each object was held on the SNIPER LDP targeting pod in TV mode for roughly 20 seconds before dimming and dropping from track. <Cite card="80e36017873c19a1" page={6} q="approximately 10" /> The aircrew explicitly distinguished the returns from stars via comparative pod imagery.

## Side-by-side: what changed, what didn't

**The structural-difference hypotheses listed in the draft of this entry can now be checked against the bytes.** The empirical result is that the *content* of the replacement is essentially the same as the original. Every "potentially sensitive" marker that drove the "tradecraft scrub" hypothesis is present in both documents:

| Specific software-load IDs (e.g., `SY0730`) | present | present |
| Narrative line: "as previously seen in PRIOR SORTIES" | present, page 6 | **also present in the replacement** |

In other words: the original entry's strongest hypothesis — that the upstream replaced this file in order to redact the EW/weapons fingerprint and the prior-sorties narrative — is **refuted by the OCR of the replacement**. The replacement carries the same operational detail. The remaining substantive difference between the two cards is the asset filename: the original was `...southern-united-states-2023.pdf` (geographic-slug mismatch with the title "Iraq, 2023"); the replacement is `...iraq-2023.pdf` (filename and title agree).

The simplest reading consistent with the evidence: the upstream replaced this file specifically to fix the filename-vs-title mismatch, NOT to re-redact the content. The "scrub" hypothesis was a plausible read of metadata alone, but the OCR side-by-side rules it out.

This is a useful integrity story in its own right: a curated archive that hypothesizes a motive should be willing to refute its own hypotheses when the bytes prove otherwise. Editorial discipline doesn't end at "we don't assert motive" — it extends to "we update our entries when later evidence narrows or refutes earlier guesses."
