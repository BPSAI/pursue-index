## What the original (`80e36017873c19a1`) contains

Six pages, MISREP format, SECRET//NOFORN. The MISREP records:

- **Originator:** 77 Expeditionary Fighter Squadron, 378 Air Expeditionary Wing, supporting Operation INHERENT RESOLVE, with 609 CAOC oversight. <Cite card="80e36017873c19a1" page={2} q="77 EFS" />
- **Platform:** a two-ship of F-16CM Fighting Falcons (paired manned-fighter sortie, in contrast to the unmanned ISR platforms in [DOW-UAP-D23](/finds/dow-d23-arabian-gulf-october-2023), [D32](/finds/dow-d32-syria-october-2024), and [D33](/finds/dow-d33-aegean-sea-october-2023)).
- **Mission type:** Defensive Counter-Air (DCA).
- **Takeoff:** ICAO `OEPS` — Prince Sultan Air Base, Saudi Arabia — at `311901:00ZMAR23`. <Cite card="80e36017873c19a1" page={1} q="OEPS" />
- **Landing:** `010148ZAPR23`. Mission duration approximately 6 hours 47 minutes.
- **Routing:** transited Iraqi airspace, then Syrian airspace; on station in killbox "ESSA" from 2058Z to 0000Z.
- **UAP event DTG:** `312302:05ZMAR23` — 31 March 2023, 23:02 Zulu.
- **Observation:** approximately 10–20 bright objects moving west-to-east at flight level above 600 (the F-16s were at FL265). Each object was held on the SNIPER LDP targeting pod in TV mode for roughly 20 seconds before dimming and dropping from track. <Cite card="80e36017873c19a1" page={6} q="approximately 10" /> The aircrew explicitly distinguished the returns from stars via comparative pod imagery.
- **Response:** no kinetic engagement; no effects on personnel; the flight returned to base normally.
- **Pattern note:** the narrative on page 6 includes language describing the formation as not having been seen in this configuration "as previously seen in PRIOR SORTIES," indicating the originator had context for repeat events.

Once the replacement file's OCR is in the index, a direct page-by-page diff against the original will show whether each of these differences narrows in the new version:

2. **Filename / title alignment.** The original asset filename was `...southern-united-states-2023.pdf`; the title is "Iraq, 2023." The replacement aligns filename and title on "iraq."
3. **The "as previously seen in PRIOR SORTIES" line on page 6.** This language places the encounter in a pattern; whether the replacement preserves the phrasing or scopes it differently is a direct factual question once the new OCR is available.
