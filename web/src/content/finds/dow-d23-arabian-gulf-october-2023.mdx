## What the document is

Card `d8e5687dc870892d` is **DOW-UAP-D23, Mission Report, United Arab Emirates, October 2023** — a 9-page MISREP submitted by USCENTCOM, declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison on 12 September 2025 and approved for release to AARO under MDR 25-0104. It originated with the 50th Attack Squadron, 432 AEW, supporting Operation SPARTAN SHIELD. The mission departed OMAM (Al Dhafra) at `240015:00ZOCT23`, came on station at 0155Z in SRO TRACK IRISH SICKLE, and landed back at OMAM at `242058:00ZOCT23` — twenty hours forty-three minutes of mission time. <Cite card="d8e5687dc870892d" page={5} q="Total Mission Time" /> (The PURSUE Release 01 CSV labels this card with `incident_date: 10/31/2023`; the MISREP's own Zulu DTGs — cited above — place the sortie on **October 24, 2023**, and that is the date this entry treats as authoritative. The manifest-field discrepancy is tracked separately as a data-quality item.)

Like D32, the same `card_id` shows up multiple times in the manifest because the corpus content-addresses by source URL: two PDF rows and two AARO video rows — **PR-26** and **PR-27** — pointing back to the same MISREP asset. Where [D32](/finds/dow-d32-syria-october-2024) was *one observation window, three FMV clips, one document*, D23 is **one mission, two distinct encounters, two videos, one document**. The encounters are entered as two separate UAP blocks — not one event split across clips — and the form's structure makes that explicit.

## The mission's running narrative

Page 1 is the running mission narrative — a numbered Zulu-time timeline. The two UAP events appear as two distinct line items:

> "(SECRET//NOFORN) AT 0241Z, 1.4a OBSERVED 1X UAP. SEE UAP LINE 1." <Cite card="d8e5687dc870892d" page={1} q="UAP LINE 1" />
>
> "(SECRET//NOFORN) AT 0322Z, 1.4a OBSERVED 1X UAP. SEE UAP LINE 2."

The narrative cross-refers each entry to its own structured UAP block: UAP LINE 1 on pages 7–8, UAP LINE 2 on pages 8–9. Two events, two form entries, two cross-references. The MISREP's authorship is committing to the count.

## The Iranian guard call — context, not setup

Earlier on that same page-1 timeline is a separate item that warrants careful framing:

> "(SECRET//REL TO USA, FVEY) AT 0145Z, 1.4a RECEIVED A PROFESSIONAL GUARD CALL. SEE GUARD CALL LINE 1." <Cite card="d8e5687dc870892d" page={1} q="GUARD CALL" />

The detail is built out on page 6. Ground Station Callsign: **IRANIAN AIR DEFENSE**. Aircraft at FL230, 031M heading, 158 KIAS. Tone logged as **Professional**. The transmitted text:

> "AT 0145Z, [callsign] RECEIVED 1X GUARD CALL FROM IRANIAN AIR DEFENSE STATING 'MAINTAIN SAFE DISTANCE FROM BORDER'. CALL WAS PROFESSIONAL. [callsign] RESPONDED 'THIS IS A COALITION AIRCRAFT CONDUCTING ROUTINE OPERATIONS IN INTERNATIONAL AIRSPACE'. AT 0145Z, THE CALLS FROM IRANIAN AIR DEFENSE CEASED." <Cite card="d8e5687dc870892d" page={6} q="MAINTAIN SAFE DISTANCE" />

The mission report records the guard call earlier in the operational window. The first UAP event is logged 56 minutes later, at 0241Z; the second at 0322Z. The two facts are on the same timeline because the timeline is chronological. The MISREP does not draw a connection between them, and neither does this entry. The guard call is documented in its own GUARDCALL block; the UAP events are documented in their own UAP blocks. They are adjacent in the form because they are adjacent in time, and that is all the document commits to.

## UAP LINE 1 — 0241Z

The first UAP block on pages 7–8 records the structured fields for the 0241Z event. The friendly aircraft is at FL243, 162 KTAS, 280-degree trajectory. The UAP-specific fields:

> "**UAP Physical State:** Solid" <Cite card="d8e5687dc870892d" page={7} q="UAP Physical State" />
>
> "**UAP Propulsion Means:** UNK"
>
> "UAP Maneuverability Observations: NONE"
>
> "UAP Signatures: THERMAL SHOWED COLD"
>
> "Observer Assessment of UAP: Benign"

The kinetic block on page 8 is partially populated: every field marked `Estimated`, with **Kinetic Velocity: 320 MPH** the only numeric value filled in. Altitude, depth, and trajectory are `UNK`. <Cite card="d8e5687dc870892d" page={8} q="320 MPH" />

The form-grammar note from D32 applies here in mirror image. **"UAP Physical State: Solid"** is a checkbox-menu field — `Solid | Gas | Plasma | UNK` — and the aircrew picked `Solid`. D32's aircrew picked `Plasma` for what they described in prose as a "ball of white light." Different observation, different checkbox. The form's range is what makes the field readable, not an independent physical-state assertion. The free-text `UAP Description` field reads `UNK`; the only specific observational language anywhere in the record comes from the paired AARO video. <Cite card="d8e5687dc870892d" page={8} q="UAP Description" />

The paired AARO **PR-26** description fills that gap from the clip side. Forty-three seconds, infrared sensor:

> "00:00-00:17: An area of contrast remains generally within the top left quarter of the display. 00:17-00:18: The sensor pans from right to left, causing the area of contrast to pass through the center of the display... 00:29: The sensor stops tracking the area of contrast, causing it to leave the sensor field-of-view on the left side of the screen. 00:30-00:43: The sensor resumes its motion relative to the background but does not reacquire the area of contrast."

PR-26 closes with the same boilerplate disclaimer that runs across the AARO catalog: the description is provided "for informational purposes only" and "should not [be interpreted] as reflecting an analytical judgment, investigative conclusion, or factual determination."

## UAP LINE 2 — 0322Z, 41 minutes later

The second UAP block on pages 8–9 records the 0322Z event. The friendly aircraft is at the same FL243, 162 KTAS, 280-degree trajectory. The structured fields read:

> "**UAP Physical State:** Solid" <Cite card="d8e5687dc870892d" page={8} q="UAP Physical State" />
>
> "**UAP Propulsion Means:** UNK"
>
> "UAP Maneuverability Observations: NONE"
>
> "UAP Signatures: UNK"
>
> "Observer Assessment of UAP: Benign"

The kinetic block on page 9 mirrors the first: every field `Estimated`, **Kinetic Velocity: 440 MPH**, altitude and trajectory `UNK`. <Cite card="d8e5687dc870892d" page={9} q="440 MPH" /> The `UAP Signatures` field, populated for the first event as `THERMAL SHOWED COLD`, is `UNK` for the second. The Description field is `UNK` again.

Two structured blocks, identical in form, populated differently in detail. The 320-vs-440-MPH delta is what the form numerically commits to between the two events. Whether the second observation is the same object as the first, a different object, or the same form-grammar applied to a different phenomenon, the MISREP does not say. There is no "Same as Initial Contact" field, and no narrative line in GENTEXT/UAP linking the two.

The paired **PR-27** clip is four minutes fifty-seven seconds, IR sensor — much longer than PR-26's forty-three. AARO records "no content" for the first 1:55, an area of contrast becoming distinguishable at 01:56, the sensor zooming in at 02:14, the area "remain[ing] generally in the center of the sensor field-of-view" through 03:26, then "sensor motion caus[ing] the area of contrast to move erratically across the display" through the end of the clip. Same observational-primitive vocabulary, same non-finding disclaimer.

## What the document does not say

Five things this MISREP and its two paired PR descriptions do **not** establish:

1. **No causal link between the Iranian guard call and the UAP events.** They appear on the same page-1 timeline because the timeline is chronological. The form does not connect them, and neither does this entry. A 56-minute gap between the guard call ending and the first UAP observation is what the document records; that is all.
2. **No propulsion or origin claim.** Both blocks read `UAP Propulsion Means: UNK` and `Observer Assessment of UAP: Benign`. The document closes the file from a tactical standpoint without committing to origin.
3. **No claim about whether the two UAPs are the same object.** Two structured entries forty-one minutes apart, two velocities, no field linking them. The form's authorship logged them as `UAP Incident` twice and stopped.
4. **The MISREP description fields are `UNK` even though video exists.** The specific observational language anywhere in the record — areas of contrast, sensor pans, durations — comes from AARO's PR-26 and PR-27, not the MISREP. The structured form admits a field for prose description and the aircrew left it `UNK`; the video is what carries the observable detail downstream.
5. **"Solid" is not a physical-state finding.** Same form-grammar caveat as D32: it is the aircrew's selection from a checkbox menu.

## Why this card is in this archive

D32 walks the MISREP's field anatomy with one observation window. D23 shows the same form being used to record **two events in one mission**, separately and structurally. Each UAP gets its own `Initial Contact DTG`, kinetic block, velocity number, and signature line; the aircrew filled out two of them, and the corpus attached one paired AARO video to each event.

A reader interested in the MISREP-anatomy frame should read D23 and [D32](/finds/dow-d32-syria-october-2024) together. Different physical-state checkbox (`Solid` vs. `Plasma`), different thermal language (`THERMAL SHOWED COLD` vs. blank), different kinematic populating (D23 commits two velocity numbers; D32 leaves the kinetic block blank). Same form, same institutional grammar, same `Observer Assessment: Benign` closure on each.
