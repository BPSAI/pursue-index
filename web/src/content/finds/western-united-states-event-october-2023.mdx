## What the documents are

Most entries in this archive walk a single card. This one walks seven, because the
Department of War released them as a set — a complete case file for one event:

- **DOW-UAP-D077** (`88479e3245f9bf15`) — *AARO Unresolved Case Analysis Update: Western United States Event*, a memorandum from the Office of the Under Secretary of War, Intelligence and Security, dated 05 June 2026.
- **DOW-UAP-D078** (`323b9443e6f5ea56`) — a *notional map* keying the event into four named incidents.
- **DOW-UAP-D079 → D083** (`c2da7eaaedbf533a`, `d7f72a4106a917f6`, `ccb9a10f4d8b2c3d`, `bf08008b8a1aa79e`, `907982561e907539`) — five *Narrative from Witness* memoranda, each authored by one of the witnessing agents.

The frame is set in the first sentence of the analysis:

> "Over a period of two days in October 2023, six federal law enforcement special agents reported observing 'orbs launching other orbs,' near..." <Cite card="88479e3245f9bf15" page={1} q="six federal law enforcement special agents" />

The precise location is withheld — the witness narratives record the coordinates as
`[REDACTED]`. What is *not* withheld is the government's own verdict on the case, stated
up front: it "remains unresolved." <Cite card="88479e3245f9bf15" page={1} q="remains unresolved" />

## The four incidents

The notional map (D078) decomposes the event into four reported incidents over several
days, the first of which is the one that names the case:

> "Incident 1: 'Orbs Launching Orbs' — Agents reported observing multiple instances of an orange 'mother orb' 'launching' smaller red..." <Cite card="323b9443e6f5ea56" page={1} q="Orbs Launching Orbs" />

The remaining incidents catalog a "Fiery Orb" and two kite-form objects. <Cite card="323b9443e6f5ea56" page={1} q="Fiery Orb" /> The map is explicitly "notional" — a scaffold for reading the five narratives, not a survey-grade plot.

## What AARO did with it

The analysis is not a believer's document, and it is not a debunk. It is a hypothesis
walk. AARO deconflicted the reports against military flight logs, radar, and ADS-B
tracking, and then took each conventional explanation in turn — ruling some out,
rating others partially plausible:

- **Misidentified military aircraft exhaust** — ruled out. <Cite card="88479e3245f9bf15" page={2} q="Ruled Out" />
- **Military aircraft dispensing flares** — partially plausible: "Historical flight logs confirm that U.S. military aircraft" were operating in the area. <Cite card="88479e3245f9bf15" page={3} q="Dispensing Flares" />
- **Misidentified celestial bodies** — considered for the "loitering" observations, assessed as unlikely under the area's light-pollution conditions. <Cite card="88479e3245f9bf15" page={4} q="celestial bodies" />

The flare hypothesis covers a majority of the observations. It does not cover the rest.
The load-bearing sentence of the whole release sits on page 2:

> "Approximately 40 percent of the reported phenomena lack a plausible explanation..." <Cite card="88479e3245f9bf15" page={2} q="40 percent of the reported phenomena" />

For that residual, AARO records that radar and ADS-B "indicate that no known" tracked
aircraft were present, and files a pending hypothesis it labels in the analysts' own
words:

> "Unrecognized Technology (Pending)" <Cite card="88479e3245f9bf15" page={4} q="Unrecognized Technology" />

"Pending" is doing real work there. The document does not assert exotic origin; it
records that the conventional explanations it tested do not close the case, and leaves
the category open.

## The agents, in their own words

The five witness narratives are first-person memoranda for record. They are worth reading
because the descriptions are specific, mutually consistent on the central event, and
careful — these are people trained to write reports, hedging where they're unsure.

**The launch.** Two separate witnesses describe the "mother orb" expelling smaller orbs.
Witness 2:

> "...it expelled three or four red lights from it. The red lights accelerated instantly and maneuvered with perfect, smooth coordination..." <Cite card="d7f72a4106a917f6" page={1} q="expelled three or four red lights" />

Witness 3, independently:

> "...it expelled three smaller red orbs. The expulsion appeared to be mechanical." <Cite card="ccb9a10f4d8b2c3d" page={1} q="appeared to" />

Witness 2 then reaches for an analogy and immediately qualifies it: the deployment
"looked like a mix of mechanical deployment and biological division; it appeared too
smooth" — and notes it "did not appear to be a defensive countermeasure." <Cite card="d7f72a4106a917f6" page={2} q="mechanical deployment and biological division" /> That is the texture of the whole file: reaching for a familiar referent, then flagging the part that doesn't fit it.

**The kite.** Witness 1 describes a different object — a translucent, kite-like form:

> "It was almost like looking at something through water where it was not completely clear." <Cite card="c2da7eaaedbf533a" page={2} q="through water" />

**The filament.** Witness 5 records a physical-residue detail that recurs in older UAP
case literature ("angel hair"): a fine, cobweb-like material seen floating in association
with the event.

> "...an identical cobweb floating nearby, but floating and flowing down toward the..." <Cite card="907982561e907539" page={3} q="identical cobweb" />

## What this is, and isn't

Read as a set, the file is unusual for three reasons. It is **recent** (October 2023,
analyzed June 2026). It is **multi-witness** by trained federal observers, with five
independent first-person narratives rather than a single account. And it is **officially
open** — the Department of War's own analysis records ~40 percent of the phenomena as
lacking a plausible explanation and the case as unresolved, after deconfliction against
the government's own flight, radar, and ADS-B data.

None of that makes it extraterrestrial, and the documents are scrupulous about not
claiming it does — "Unrecognized Technology" is logged as *pending*, not concluded. What
the file does establish, on the government's own letterhead, is narrower and harder to
wave off: there is a 2023 multi-witness event that the agency tasked with explaining it
cannot, yet, fully explain. Everything above is quoted from the released documents and
hash-pinned to the cards for verification.
